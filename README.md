# AirChess

**Your table is the board. Your friends are anywhere.**

AirChess drops a full-size, real-feeling chess set onto any table, desk, or floor through your phone's camera, then lets you play it: alone, against the computer, with a coach at your shoulder, or against a friend across town.

**[Download for Android](https://github.com/Monster0506/airchess/releases/latest/download/airchess.apk)**. Free, no account, no ads.

## Play on any surface

Point your phone at a table. A glowing outline finds the surface. Tap, and a polished wooden board with all 32 pieces settles into place, anchored to the real world. Walk around it. Lean in. Look at the position from your opponent's side. It stays exactly where you put it.

No AR on your phone? The same board is waiting in 3D.

## Touch the pieces

Tap a piece and its legal moves light up right on the board. Tap a square and the piece glides there, with a gentle buzz in your hand. Captures, castling, en passant, promotion: every rule of chess, enforced perfectly, so you only ever think about the game.

## A grandmaster coach on the table

Stuck? Tap **Hint**. Stockfish, one of the strongest chess engines ever built, reads the position and draws its best move as a glowing arrow across the board itself, with an evaluation of who's winning. Turn on threat view to see which of your pieces are under attack before it's too late.

## Take on the computer

Pick your side and a difficulty from Easy to Max, and Stockfish plays back. Made a mistake? Undo takes back your move and its reply, so you can try again.

## Challenge a friend, anywhere

Create a game and send an invite. One tap opens it on your friend's phone, or in any web browser if they don't have the app yet. They place their own board on their own table, and you play live, each from your own side of the board.

Pick a time control from 1-minute bullet to 15+10 rapid, choose your color, and watch the clocks tick down. Offer a draw, resign with dignity, or demand a rematch.

## See their board, not just their moves

Tap **Share view** and you see your opponent's board exactly as they do: their room, their table, and the pieces standing on it. Their view waits in a corner of your screen; tap it to go full screen, with your own view tucked in the corner, like a video call across the board.

Sharing is off until you turn it on, either of you can stop it at any time, and nothing is recorded. There's no microphone, and only the board view is sent, never your other apps.

## Pick up where you left off

Close the app mid-game, and your game waits for you on the home screen. Dropped connection during an online match? AirChess reconnects you to the same game, clocks and all.

## Play in your browser

No phone handy? Play right in the browser, in 2D or 3D: pass and play, take on Stockfish, or join a friend who's playing on the app.

## Built for the moment

- Instant setup: open the app, find a table, play.
- No accounts, no ads, no menus to wade through.
- Every rule, every draw condition, handled for you.
- Full move history at a glance.
- Works on Android 8.0 and later. AR on ARCore-supported phones, 3D everywhere else.

**AirChess. Chess, wherever you are.**
